How to Play

Phase 1: Place your pieces

  • X goes first, then players take turns.
  • Click an empty cell to place a piece.
  • Each player can place only 3 pieces.

Phase 2: Move your pieces

  • After all 3 pieces are placed, drag one of your pieces to an empty cell.
  • You can move up, down, left or right by one cell.
  • Any piece can move into the center cell if it is empty.
  • A piece in the center can move to any empty cell.

How to win

  • Get 3 of your pieces in a row: horizontal, vertical or diagonal.
  • Press Reset to start a new game.
